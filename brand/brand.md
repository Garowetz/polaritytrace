## Type

- **Titles:** Fractul Regular. All caps is allowed for emphasis.
- **Subtitles and sub-brand descriptors:** Charter Italic.
- **Body:** Roobert (Regular, Italic, Bold). The signature line is Roobert
  Medium.
- **Substitutes** when the brand fonts aren't available, such as Word or email:
  **Arial** for Fractul and Roobert, **Georgia Italic** for Charter.
